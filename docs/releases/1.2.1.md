cry of fear story co-op 1.2.1 is out

**1.2.1**
- some people got a trojan warning from their antivirus on the app. it wasn't a trojan, it was a false positive. the app was one exe that unpacked itself every time it started, which looks suspicious to antivirus programs
- the app is now a normal program with its own folder of files called coop-app. keep it next to CoF Story Co-op.exe. it has its own icon and version details
- nothing changed in the game. 1.1 to 1.2.1 all play together

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
