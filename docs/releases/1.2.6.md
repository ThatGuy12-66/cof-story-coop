cry of fear story co-op 1.2.6 is out

**1.2.6**
had the whole thing code reviewed top to bottom and fixed what came up:
- if two of you end up on the same spot (like the little dark room after a door, or after pressing J) you can actually use the way out now. the game was ignoring everyone on that spot until you moved apart, which is probably some of the "stuck in the black room" stuff
- opening a door right where you just arrived doesn't take you through the level exit twice anymore
- respawning or pressing J next to someone standing in a level exit doesn't send everyone back a level anymore
- if everyone dies before a new game or chapter makes its first checkpoint, the level starts over instead of loading your last session's checkpoint
- two people with the same name don't turn checkpoints off anymore (joining while the host is loading a level could do that)
- ladders don't show as busy when the last person who climbed them is in a cutscene
- if you couldn't follow someone through a door you still get the press J message even if they go straight through another door
- when you join, the app tells you if the host is on an older version. almost every fix runs on the host's side, so if the host didn't update, you don't get them
- 1.1 to 1.2.6 all play together, but everyone should update, the host especially

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
