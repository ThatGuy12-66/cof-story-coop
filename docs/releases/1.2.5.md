cry of fear story co-op 1.2.5 is out

**1.2.5**
co-op runs the game as a multiplayer game and the original game turns a bunch of single player stuff off in multiplayer. went through all of it and brought back what was missing:
- quick dual wield works now. hit two quick slot keys at the same time (like 1 and 2) with two things that go together, like a pistol and the flashlight. tap one by itself to go back to just that weapon so you can reload
- monsters can't hit you through walls and doors anymore. the taller's stomp could kill you through a wall too, that's fixed
- you limp when you're low on health like in single player
- the control hints show up again (sprint, dodge, quick slots, saving, the phone etc)
- taking a second syringe too fast gives you the morphine trip again
- your quick slot pictures don't disappear after going through a door anymore (that was only happening to guests)
- everyone gets the new objective popup, not just one person
- nightmare difficulty uses its actual stamina rules now
- your first person arms are simon's (or your costume's) instead of the police guy's
- smaller stuff: pings show in the player list, the glock ejects shells, the intro card is in your language, no random health bar in the last ending, and someone respawning doesn't wipe everyone's messages anymore
- 1.1 to 1.2.5 all play together, but everyone should update

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
