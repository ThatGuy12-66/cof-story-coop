cry of fear story co-op 1.1.7 is out

**1.1.7**
- level changes are faster for players who join. the host now sends 10 pieces of the new level's setup at a time instead of one, same amount TCP starts with. each piece still fits in one normal packet
- bigger difference when the host and joining player are farther apart. loading the level from disk takes the same amount of time
- your microphone stays open through level changes now, no more closing and reopening it. also stops a small memory leak
- 1.1 to 1.1.7 all play together. faster level changes are in the host's game, so the host should update

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
