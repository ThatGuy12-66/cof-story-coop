cry of fear story co-op 1.2.8 is out

**1.2.8**
the windows defender fix:
- the game engine was starting a windows tool (WMIC) in the background on every launch to read hardware serial numbers for its player ID. that's exactly what some malware does, so defender sometimes killed the game ("game ended") and quarantined CoFLaunchApp.exe. the game doesn't start any other program anymore, it reads the ID itself
- a controller stick that drifts can't keep hiding the mouse cursor in the menus anymore
- if your mouse cursor goes missing in the menus, your log now says why, send it to us
- 1.1 to 1.2.8 all play together

if defender quarantined the game before: restore CoFLaunchApp.exe from Protection history (or hit Repair in the app), then update

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
