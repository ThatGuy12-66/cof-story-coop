cry of fear story co-op 1.1.9 is out

**1.1.9**
- polish pass focused on how co-op looks and feels with other players
- weapons, ammo and meds placed in levels are per-player now. when one person grabs something, everyone else can still grab their own. story items like keys and fuses are still shared
- u can see other players' gas mask (the night vision mask) when they wear it. setup/update builds the mask model on ur pc. if that game file is modded, only the mask is left out
- ladders work better. other players see u climbing instead of a floating pair of hands, and climbing is smoother for players who join. holding W when u grab a ladder works
- other players move smoother, with more updates, no snapping back when someone comes into view, and steadier running. sprinting shows now, and AI companions sprint to keep up
- c_lake boat: the rower's body doesn't stay standing on the shore anymore
- story fixes: the chapter 8 strangle goes to the player who turned the last valve, leaving during the ending house scene doesn't trap everyone, intro logos show for everyone, partners get out of the falling carriage in c_bridge, one-way teleports bring partners along, and two exits in the apartment levels don't strand the party anymore
- the asylum puzzle, broken phone, asylum telephone, and max health change work for everyone now
- items: fuses always count right, someone leaving while holding the P345 or sledgehammer doesn't duplicate or lose it, and chapter starts keep weapons through an early checkpoint reload. chapter 4 doesn't need the end of chapter 3 replayed anymore, and ending 4 partners get weapons
- app: setup works with a steam folder reached through a link and refuses Program Files and OneDrive. a router port left open by a crash gets cleaned up, and a newer copy tells u to use its shortcut
- 1.1 to 1.1.9 all play together. most fixes are in the host's game, so the host should update. everyone should update to see the gas mask and get smoother movement

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
