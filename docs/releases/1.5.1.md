cry of fear story co-op 1.5.1 is out

**1.5.1**
- new player tools, made for this version of the mod:
  - F3: unstuck. gets you out of a wall or a prop, or puts you next to your partner
  - G: ping. tells everyone where you are (your name and how far away)
  - V: scare the nearest teammate. it's a text message for now, no laugh sound yet
  - Q: drop one box of your spare ammo for someone else
  - F6: hide or show the HUD
  - T: spray your own picture (pick a .png or .bmp in the app)
  - these keys only get set if your own controls leave them free, nothing you bound yourself is changed
- new Comfort row in the app: field of view, head bob, the faint light around you in the dark, black and white, hide the HUD, and your spray. they apply to every game the app starts, hosting or joining
- you get told when a teammate is down, and the objective shows again when you join late, after a respawn and after unstuck
- the app remembers the last game you joined over the network and offers to rejoin it (Dismiss hides it)
- for hosts: revive time, respawn time and outlines on far away teammates are settings now (defaults 5 s, 10 s and off). no buttons for them in the app yet
- 1.1 to 1.5.1 all play together. unstuck, ping, scare, spare ammo, black and white and the host settings need the host on 1.5.1

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept

**known issues**
- the tools haven't been tried in a real game yet, if one misbehaves tell me on discord
- a respawn time of 60 seconds or more still waits the game's normal 60 seconds
- there's no way to clear a spray once you picked one yet, and the new app text is english only for now
