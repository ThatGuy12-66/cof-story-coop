small one:
- if your antivirus deletes CoFLaunchApp.exe (the thing that starts the game) the app now tells you that and offers Repair, instead of the windows "cannot find the file specified" error. if that happens, restore it from your antivirus quarantine or hit Repair, and add an exception for the game folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\CoF-Story-Coop) so it stays
- 1.1 to 1.2.7 all play together

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
