cry of fear story co-op 1.5 is out

**1.5**
- there's a language setting now. the list at the bottom of the app sets the language for the app and the game together: english, deutsch, español, français, nederlands, norsk, polski, русский, svenska and 简体中文 (simplified chinese). the game's own options > game > language is the same setting, change it in either place and the other one follows. first time you open it, it goes with your windows language
- russian and simplified chinese are new and fully translated: subtitles, notes, hints, endings, phone messages, item names, map text (doors, chapter titles, save points), the menus, the co-op pages and messages, and the pictures with english on them (item and weapon cards, the inventory, the yes/no questions, game over, the intros, signs, the writing on the walls)
- german, spanish, french, dutch, norwegian and swedish now also get item names, map text and the game's messages, which cry of fear only ever showed in english. polish gets the co-op pages
- chinese characters show up everywhere now: menus, subtitles, notes, chat and player names
- the translations were drafted by AI and haven't been checked by native speakers yet. if something reads wrong, tell me on discord (the line and what it should say) and i'll fix it
- everyone picks their own language. some map messages come from the host's game so they show in the host's language
- 1.1 to 1.5 all play together

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
