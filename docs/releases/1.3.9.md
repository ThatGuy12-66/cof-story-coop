cry of fear story co-op 1.3.9 is out

**1.3.9**
- cry of fear's own co-op can start the co-op campaign from any of its 4 maps again. Co-op campaign part 2, 3 or 4 are back in its list in the game's Co-op menu. later parts have no lobby and everyone starts with that part's weapons. the old app page had these and they got lost when it went away in 1.3.7
- weapons after a revive: if you respawned (jump or the respawn timer) while someone was reviving you, your guns never fired again that life and monsters ignored you. fixed, your weapons work as soon as you're back
- crash after dropping a weapon: the game kept pointing at a weapon after you dropped it, and a later weapon switch (getting up after a knockdown, putting night vision away etc) could grab the one on the floor and crash. those leftovers get cleared now. this one was found in the game's code, nobody's caught it happening yet, so if it still crashes around a revive or a drop send your log in the discord
- in cry of fear's own co-op the story's fixes for a stuck reviver, a cut-short syringe heal and a fall a cutscene interrupted now run too
- both co-op modes get the weapon fixes. 1.1 to 1.3.9 all play together, the host should update since the fixes are in the host's game

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
