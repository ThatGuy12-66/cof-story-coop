cry of fear story co-op 1.1.6 is out

**1.1.6**
- fixes from a couple co-op sessions with Xavier, ty for the logs
- hands no longer disappear after going through a door into the next level
- flashlight (F) stays on through level changes, and one press always turns it on or off
- everyone can see each other's flashlight again after a level change
- no more bouncing between two levels. the way back only takes you through its door, and if you arrive standing in it, step out first
- players going through a door during a level change no longer get stuck frozen and invisible
- 1.1 to 1.1.6 all play together. the fixes are in the host's game, so the host should update

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
