cry of fear story co-op 1.3.7 is out

**1.3.7**
- the app is basically just a play button now. hit Play and the game opens on its main menu, and Co-op (under New Game) has everything the app's play solo / host / join / own co-op screens had. the app still does setup and updates, and while you play it shows your join code and keeps your router port open
- updates are way quicker now. the update used to remake the simon and weapon models, the inventory art and all 29 joined story maps every single time, which took a few minutes. now it checks them and keeps whatever didn't change, so an update is basically the download and the new engine
- the joined maps get kept starting with this update. the models and art get made one more time this time (a few seconds) and get kept after that
- 1.1 to 1.3.7 all play together

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
