cry of fear story co-op 1.1.8 is out

**1.1.8**
- debug pass over the whole story for stuff that breaks with more than one player. planning for this to be the last update for a while
- shared items work now: the glock flashlight attachment (taclight), phone battery, and gates that need two fuses
- chapter starts work now. everyone gets the weapons the story would have given by then, including the phone for the chapter 2 door code and guns for chapter 8 and the endings
- the chapter 7 doctor fights back now. on a chapter start he couldn't be killed
- chapter 5 starts where it should, instead of halfway through chapter 4
- if a player misses a level change or is still loading, they get told again and follow instead of timing out and getting banned
- walk-in exits work like every other exit now. the game waits for the party, J goes without the stragglers, and nobody lands inside a wall or bounces straight back
- doors, teleports, and respawns won't pull you through a door you walked away from, send you after someone into a deadly drop, or put you inside a wall
- boat on c_lake: everyone comes to the dock, and someone still rowing when the level changes gets let out first
- story scenes work for everyone now, including the wardrobe, the grey fade in ending 4, fades that never lifted for other players, the c_down ladder, and a few others
- the end of the story takes everyone back to the menu
- app: added a Repair button when your copy fails its check, setup can clean up after itself and try again, modded game files get caught before the 5 GB copy, J/H/Enter keep working after Options > Use defaults, and the "No answer" message mentions Windows Firewall
- 1.1 to 1.1.8 all play together. most fixes are in the host's game, so the host should update

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
