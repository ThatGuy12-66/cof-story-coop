cry of fear story co-op 1.3.8 is out

**1.3.8**
- whatever you've unlocked in normal cry of fear now carries over to co-op. every time you hit Play the app copies your unlocks (endings, the bonus room weapons, dev commentary, all of it) from your own game into the co-op copy. before it only had what you had unlocked when you set it up. it only adds stuff, so anything you unlock in co-op stays, and your own game doesn't get touched
- "bonus room unlocked" is now "open the bonus room". unticked, the bonus room and its stuff are open as far as the host has unlocked them (it used to just be shut). ticked opens all of it for everyone like before. it goes by the host's unlocks since the host's game runs the level
- Y is text chat and K is push to talk for voice. they've worked since 1.0 but weren't listed anywhere, they're in the readme and the app now
- 1.1 to 1.3.8 all play together, the host should update for the unlocks

**updating**
- on 1.1.2 or newer, open the app and click Update now
- on 1.1.1 or older, extract the new zip, run CoF Story Co-op.exe and click Update
- saves and settings are kept
