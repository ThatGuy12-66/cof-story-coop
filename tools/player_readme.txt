Cry of Fear Co-op 1.5.2

Play the Cry of Fear campaign together (2 to 4 players), or alone with AI companions.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.2
- The host's phone light turns with the camera again. In co-op, the host's phone light
  stayed at the chest and only lit up the area around you, as if the phone were in a bag.
  Now it points where you look.
- The host's phone light also no longer takes over a guest's phone light while it is on.
- This was an old clash in Cry of Fear's own game code, there since the first co-op
  version. It also hit Play with AI and the first player on a dedicated server.
- This fix hasn't been tried in a real game yet. If the phone light still misbehaves, tell
  us on Discord.
- 1.1 to 1.5.2 can play together. The fix is in each player's own game, so everyone
  should update.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.1
- Player tools, new in this version: F3 gets you unstuck (out of a wall or a prop, or next
  to your partner); G tells everyone where you are (your name and how far away);
  V scares the nearest teammate (a message only, no sound yet); Q drops one box of your
  spare ammo for someone else; F6 hides or shows the HUD; T sprays your own picture.
  These keys are only set where your own controls leave them free.
- A Comfort row in the app: field of view, head bob, a faint light around you in the
  dark, black and white, hide the HUD, and your spray (a .png or .bmp picture). They
  apply to every game the app starts, hosting or joining.
- "Name is down" when a teammate goes down, and the objective is shown again when you
  join late, after a respawn and after getting unstuck.
- The app remembers the last game you joined over the network and offers to rejoin it
  (Dismiss hides it).
- For hosts: the revive time, the respawn time and outlines on teammates far away are now
  settings the game reads when a co-op game starts (by default 5 seconds, 10 seconds and
  off). The app has no buttons for them yet.
- These tools were made for this version and haven't been tried in a real game yet. If
  one misbehaves, tell us on Discord.
- 1.1 to 1.5.1 can play together. Unstuck, ping, scare, spare ammo, black and white and
  the host settings work when the host has 1.5.1.

YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, and Cry of Fear installed from Steam (it's free). Keep Steam running.
- About 6 GB of free disk space.

INSTALL
1. Extract this zip to a normal folder, such as Downloads (not into the game folder).
   Keep the coop-app folder next to "CoF Story Co-op.exe": it holds the app's own files.
2. Double-click "CoF Story Co-op.exe". Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Click "Set up". The app finds your game through Steam (or use Browse to pick the
   Cry of Fear folder) and makes a separate copy for co-op. Your Steam game, saves and
   settings are not changed.
4. Afterwards, start it with the "Cry of Fear Co-op" shortcut on your desktop or in the
   Start menu.

PLAY
Click Play in the app. Cry of Fear opens at its main menu: pick Co-op, right under New Game.
- Host co-op: a new game or a chapter, for friends. Send them the join code the app shows
  (the game's Host co-op page shows it too). If they can't connect, your router didn't
  open the port by itself: forward UDP 27015 (or the port set in the app) to your PC in
  the router, or all use a VPN such as Tailscale, ZeroTier or Radmin and share that
  address. If Windows Firewall asks about Cry of Fear, allow it.
- Join co-op: paste the host's join code (or address) and their password, if any.
- Continue co-op: go on from a co-op checkpoint, hosted for friends or with AI companions.
- Play with AI: you and one to three AI companions.
- Cry of Fear's own co-op: the game's separate co-op campaign, manhunt or survival maps
  (2 to 4 players). The co-op campaign can start from any of its four maps; a later one
  has no lobby and everyone starts with that part's weapons. In a lobby, everyone presses
  the attack button when ready; the game starts a few seconds after the last one. The
  story's extras (AI companions, merged maps, checkpoints, J to join) are off in this mode.
Everyone needs the same version of Cry of Fear Co-op. Keep the app's window open while you
play: it keeps your router port open and shows your join code.

Start a new game (the intro), any chapter, or continue from a co-op checkpoint.
The game makes a co-op checkpoint by itself after every level change, and the host's tape
recorder saves are checkpoints too: pick one in Continue co-op's list.
The bonus room (FAMAS, axe and more) is open as far as the host has unlocked it in Cry of
Fear; tick "Open the bonus room" to open all of it for everyone.
Starting a later chapter lays its weapons out next to you, and O opens the wardrobe.
Dual wield (two pistols, or a pistol and a light) works from the inventory menu.
"Merged maps" (on by default) loads neighbouring levels as one map, so there are fewer
loading screens. If a merged level misbehaves, the host can untick it and start that
chapter again.

CO-OP KEYS
J      join your partner (when the screen says "Press J to join")
Enter  skip a cutscene for everyone (press again to watch the rest)
H      tell the AI companions to wait or to follow
O      open the wardrobe (outfits) anywhere, when the bonus room is open
Y      text chat
K      voice chat (hold to talk)
F3     unstuck
G      ping: tell everyone where you are
V      scare the nearest teammate
Q      drop a box of spare ammo
F6     hide or show the HUD
T      spray your picture
Space  when dead: respawn next to your partner after about 10 seconds

UPDATES
The app checks for a newer version when it opens. If there is one, click "Update now":
it downloads the new version, closes, and the new version updates your copy.
Your saves and settings are kept. (A newer zip's "CoF Story Co-op.exe" and its
"Update" button do the same.)

UNINSTALL
Delete the CoF-Story-Coop folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\CoF-Story-Coop unless you chose
another place) and the two "Cry of Fear Co-op" shortcuts.

KNOWN ISSUES
- At the college, the doors of the classroom where you find the screwdriver once kept
  bringing a player back into the same room (reported once, not seen since). If it happens
  to you, please report it on Discord with your log: this version records what those doors did.

REPORTING BUGS
Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/4uwV6jmmY7 ("Report a bug" in the app opens it).
Say what happened, the map or the place, roughly when, and whether "Merged maps" was on,
and attach the log ("Open logs folder" in the app; the log may show your Windows user name).
If Setup or Update stops, attach cof-story-setup.log from the co-op copy folder.
Never post your password or join code publicly.
