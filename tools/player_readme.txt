Cry of Fear Co-op 1.3.6

Play the Cry of Fear campaign together (2 to 4 players), or alone with AI companions.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.6
- Co-op is in the game's own main menu now. Click Open the game (next to Your name in this
  app), then Co-op, right under New Game: Host co-op, Join co-op, Continue co-op, Play with
  AI, and Cry of Fear's own co-op (it was under Extras). Each starts the same game this
  app's buttons start, with the same choices.
- Join co-op in the game takes a join code as well as an address, and a password.
- While the game is open, this app shows your join code and asks your router to open the
  port, as when you host from here. The game's Host co-op page shows the code too.
- The app's own Play solo, Host a game, Join a game and Cry of Fear's own co-op work as
  before.
- 1.1 to 1.3.6 can play together.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.5
- Cry of Fear's own co-op mode: the players who joined no longer drop out at every level
  change or door and have to join again. The host's way of sending them to the next map
  never reached their game, so they sat on the old map until the host timed them out. They
  now go on to the next map with the host, the way the story does it.
- In that mode every player now gets their own copy of each weapon, ammo box and medicine
  a map puts down, as in the story. Before, whoever got there first took the only one.
- The host needs 1.3.5 for both. 1.1 to 1.3.5 can play together.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.4
- Everyone rides a wheelchair in the fight at the end of Ending 4. The game put only the
  first player in one, and the others fought on foot. Now every player gets a chair of
  their own and its gun, the book Simons go for whoever is nearest, and the ride ends for
  everyone when the boss dies. The host needs 1.3.4 for this.
- No co-op checkpoint is made during that ride any more: the chair doesn't keep who sits in
  it, so a restart from one could leave the riders stuck. If everyone dies there, the game
  goes back to the checkpoint before that level.
- 1.1 to 1.3.4 can play together.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.3
- Pick the save to continue from. Play solo and Host a game list every co-op checkpoint in
  your copy under "Continue from", newest first: the last level change and each tape
  recorder save, with the time it was saved. Continue used to load only the newest one.
- Cry of Fear's own co-op mode is in the app: the co-op campaign (from its lobby, or
  straight into part 2, 3 or 4), the manhunt campaign and Survival 1 to 4, as the game made
  them, for 2 to 4 players. Choose "Cry of Fear's own co-op" on the main screen; friends
  join with Join a game as usual. It runs on this app's engine, like the story, so the
  original game's multiplayer doesn't have to work on your PC. The host needs 1.3.3 for it.
- 1.1 to 1.3.3 can play together.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.2
- Antivirus programs took the small program that starts the game (CoFLaunchApp.exe) for a
  trojan: 16 of 71 scanners on VirusTotal flagged it, Microsoft Defender among them, and
  some removed it. It was never malware: its icon was stored compressed, which made half
  the file look like a hidden packed payload to their guessing, and it ran with its
  window hidden. It is now built as an ordinary Windows program with a plain icon:
  Microsoft Defender, Bitdefender (and the scanners built on it) and Trend Micro no longer
  flag it, 2 small scanners of 68 still do. Nothing else changed.
- 1.1 to 1.3.2 can play together.

YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, and Cry of Fear installed from Steam (it's free). Keep Steam running.
- About 6 GB of free disk space.

INSTALL
1. Extract this zip to a normal folder, such as Downloads (not into the game folder).
   Keep the coop-app folder next to "CoF Story Co-op.exe": it holds the app's own files.
2. Double-click "CoF Story Co-op.exe". Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Click "Set up". The app finds your game through Steam (or use Browse to pick the
   Cry of Fear folder) and makes a separate copy for co-op. Your Steam game, saves and
   settings are not changed.
4. Afterwards, start it with the "Cry of Fear Co-op" shortcut on your desktop or in the
   Start menu.

PLAY
- Play solo: you and one to three AI companions.
- Host a game: friends join you. Send them the join code the app shows.
  If they can't connect, your router didn't open the port by itself: forward UDP 27015
  to your PC in the router, or all use a VPN such as Tailscale, ZeroTier or Radmin and
  share that address. If Windows Firewall asks about Cry of Fear, allow it.
- Join a game: paste the host's join code (or address) and their password, if any.
- Cry of Fear's own co-op: host the game's separate co-op campaign, manhunt or survival
  maps (2 to 4 players). In a lobby, everyone presses the attack button when ready; the
  game starts a few seconds after the last one. The story's extras (AI companions, merged
  maps, checkpoints, J to join) are off in this mode.
Everyone needs the same version of Cry of Fear Co-op.

Start a new game (the intro), any chapter, or Continue from a co-op checkpoint.
The game makes a co-op checkpoint by itself after every level change, and the host's tape
recorder saves are checkpoints too: pick one in the list next to "Continue from".
Tick "Bonus room unlocked" to open the bonus room (FAMAS, axe and more) for everyone.
Starting a later chapter lays its weapons out next to you, and O opens the wardrobe.
Dual wield (two pistols, or a pistol and a light) works from the inventory menu.
"Merged maps" (on by default) loads neighbouring levels as one map, so there are fewer
loading screens. If a merged level misbehaves, the host can untick it and start that
chapter again.

CO-OP KEYS
J      join your partner (when the screen says "Press J to join")
Enter  skip a cutscene for everyone (press again to watch the rest)
H      tell the AI companions to wait or to follow
O      open the wardrobe (outfits) anywhere, when the bonus room is ticked
Space  when dead: respawn next to your partner after about 10 seconds

UPDATES
The app checks for a newer version when it opens. If there is one, click "Update now":
it downloads the new version, closes, and the new version updates your copy.
Your saves and settings are kept. (A newer zip's "CoF Story Co-op.exe" and its
"Update" button do the same.)

UNINSTALL
Delete the CoF-Story-Coop folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\CoF-Story-Coop unless you chose
another place) and the two "Cry of Fear Co-op" shortcuts.

KNOWN ISSUES
- At the college, the doors of the classroom where you find the screwdriver once kept
  bringing a player back into the same room (reported once, not seen since). If it happens
  to you, please report it on Discord with your log: this version records what those doors did.

REPORTING BUGS
Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/4uwV6jmmY7 ("Report a bug" in the app opens it).
Say what happened, the map or the place, roughly when, and whether "Merged maps" was on,
and attach the log ("Open logs folder" in the app; the log may show your Windows user name).
If Setup or Update stops, attach cof-story-setup.log from the co-op copy folder.
Never post your password or join code publicly.
